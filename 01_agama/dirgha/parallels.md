# [The Long Discourses](index.html)
## Table of Parallel Texts

The table below lists major parallels to <em>sūtra</em>s found in the <cite>Dīrgha Āgama</cite>. These are typically different versions of a given <em>sūtra</em> that are found in other Buddhist sources. In a few cases, a large section of a <em>sūtra</em> is reproduced as a separate text.

Most <em>sūtra</em>s in DĀ have parallels that cover all or large portions of their material. DĀ 2 is a special case in that it is both very large and composed of many sections that exist as independent texts elsewhere. I’ve created a separate table that breaks it down by section to document its parallels as fully as possible.
