    <td id='p1'>1. Thus I have heard:<sup id="ref1"><a href="#n1">1</a></sup> One time, the Buddha was staying in the land of Anomiya of Maineya.<sup id="ref2"><a href="#n2">2</a></sup> He was accompanied by a great assembly of 1,250 monks.</td>

<tr>
  <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a11'></td>
  <td class='subheading'>Why Sunakṣatra Returned to the Lay Life</td>
</tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a11'>爾時，世尊著
衣持鉢，入阿㝹夷城乞食。爾時，世尊默自
念言：「我今乞食，於時如早，今宜往詣<!-- [9] -->房伽
婆梵志園觀。<!-- [10] -->比丘<!-- [11] -->須時至，然後乞食。」</td>
    <td id='p2'>2. It was then that the Bhagavān put on his robe and took his bowl into the city of Anomiya to solicit alms. The Bhagavān then silently thought, “It’s too early in the day to solicit alms now. It would be better to visit the Wanderer Bhārgava’s park. I’ll solicit alms when it’s the right time for a monk to do so.”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a14'>爾時，世
尊即詣彼園，時，彼梵志遙見佛來，即起奉迎，
共相問訊，言：「善來，瞿曇！不面來久，今以何
緣乃能屈顧？唯願瞿曇就此處坐！」爾時，世
尊即就其<!-- [12] -->坐。</td>
    <td id='p3'>3. The Bhagavān then went to that park. The wanderer there saw the Buddha coming from a distance, got up to meet him respectfully, and exchanged greetings with him. “Welcome, Gautama! It’s been a while since you’ve been here. What brings you to visit me? Please, Gautama, make yourself a seat here!” The Bhagavān then prepared a seat for himself.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a18'>時，彼梵志於一面坐，白世
尊言：「先夜<!-- [13] -->隸車子<!-- [14] -->善宿比丘來至我所，
語我言：『大師！我不於佛所修梵行也。所
以然者？佛疎外我。』彼人見向說瞿曇過，雖
有此言，我亦不受。」</td>
    <td id='p4'>4. The wanderer sat to one side and said to the Bhagavān, “Last night, the Licchavi monk Sunakṣatra visited me.<sup id="ref3"><a href="#n3">3</a></sup> He said, ‘Great teacher, I’m not practicing the religious life under the Buddha. Why is that? The Buddha is estranged from me.’ That man openly spoke of you as mistaken, though I didn’t accept it on his word.”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a22'>佛告梵志：「彼善宿所言，
知汝不受耳。昔我一時在毗舍離獼猴池側
集法堂上，時，此善宿來至我所，語我言：『如
來外我，我不於如來所修梵行也。』</td>
    <td id='p5'>5. The Buddha told the wanderer, “I knew you wouldn’t accept what Sunakṣatra says. Once when I was staying near Monkey Lake of Vaiśālī, there was a meeting in the Dharma hall. This Sunakṣatra came to me and said, ‘The Tathāgata is estranged from me. I won’t practice the religious life under Gautama.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a25'>我時告
曰：『汝何故言：我不於如來所修梵行，如來
外我耶？』</td>
    <td id='p6'>6. “I asked him, ‘Why do you say, “I won’t practice the religious life under the Tathāgata because the he is estranged from me”?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a27'>善宿報我言：『如來不為我現神
足變化。』</td>
    <td>“Sunakṣatra replied to me, ‘The Tathāgata hasn’t demonstrated miraculous abilities or miracles for me.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66a28'>「時，我語言：『吾可請汝於我法中淨
修梵行，當為汝現神足耶？』<!-- [15] -->復當語我：『如
來當為我現神足變化，然後我當修梵行
耶？』</td>
    <td id='p7'>7. “I said, ‘Did I ask that you to purely cultivate the religious life according to my teaching if I demonstrate my miraculous abilities for you? Did you say to me, “I will practice the religious life after the Tathāgata demonstrates miraculous abilities and miracles for me”?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b2'>時，善宿報我言：『不也。世尊！』</td>
    <td>“Sunakṣatra replied, ‘No, Bhagavān.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b2'>佛告善宿：『我
亦不語汝言：汝於我法中淨修梵行，當為
汝現神足變化；汝亦不言為我現神足者，
當修梵行。</td>
    <td id='p8'>8. “I told Sunakṣatra, ‘But I didn’t say to you, ‘You practice the religious life under my teaching, and I will demonstrate miraculous abilities and miracles for you.’ Nor did you say, ‘I’ll cultivate the religious life when you demonstrate miraculous abilities for me.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b5'>云何，善宿！如汝意者，謂如來
能現神足、為不能現耶？我所說法，彼法能
得出要，盡苦際不耶？』</td>
    <td id='p9'>9. “‘How is it, Sunakṣatra? What do you think? Can the Tathāgata demonstrate miraculous abilities, or can’t he? Can one use the Dharma that I teach to escape and reach the end of suffering?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b7'>善宿白佛言：『如是，世
尊！如來能現神足，非為不能，所可說法，
能得出要，盡諸苦際，非為不盡。』</td>
    <td>“Sunakṣatra said to me, ‘Yes, Bhagavān. The Tathāgata can demonstrate miraculous abilities. It’s not that he can’t. One can use the Dharma that he teaches to escape and reach the end of suffering. It’s not that they can’t.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b9'>『是故，善
宿！我所說法修梵行者，能現神足，非為
不能，出要離苦，非不能離，汝於此法欲
何所求？』</td>
    <td id='p10'>10. “‘So, Sunakṣatra, someone who cultivates the religious life under my teaching can demonstrate miraculous abilities. It’s not that they can’t. They can escape from suffering. It’s not that they can’t escape it. What do you seek from this teaching?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b12'>「善宿言：『世尊！不能隨時教我，我父
祕術，世尊盡知，悋不教我。』</td>
    <td>“‘Sunakṣatra said, ‘Bhagavān, you aren’t able to teach me the mystery of my father at the appropriate time. The Bhagavān fully knows it, but he holds back and doesn’t teach it to me.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b13'>佛言：『善宿！我頗
曾言：汝於我法中修梵行者，教汝父術耶？
汝頗復言：教我父術者，當於佛所修梵
行耶？』</td>
    <td id='p11'>11. “‘I said, ‘Sunakṣatra, did I say before, “If you practice the religious life under my teaching, I will teach you the mystery of your father?” Did you say, “Teach me the mystery of my father, and I will practice the religious life under the Buddha.”’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b16'>答曰：『不也。』</td>
    <td>“He answered, ‘No.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b16'>『是故，善宿！我先無此言，汝
亦無言，今者何故作此語耶？云何，善宿！汝
謂如來能說汝父祕術，為不能說耶？所
可說法，能得出要，盡苦際不耶？』</td>
    <td id='p12'>12. “‘Therefore, Sunakṣatra, I said none of these things before, and you said none of them. Why are you saying this now? How is it, Sunakṣatra? Do you think the Tathāgata can teach you the mystery of your father, or can’t he? Can one use the Dharma he teaches to escape and reach the end of suffering?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b19'>善宿報言：
『如來能說父之祕術，非為不能；說法出要，
能盡苦際，非為不能。』</td>
    <td>“Sunakṣatra replied, ‘The Tathāgata can teach me the mystery of my father. It’s not that he can’t. One can use his Dharma to escape and reach the end of suffering. It’s not that they can’t.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b21'>佛告善宿：『若我能
說汝父祕術，亦能說法出要離苦，汝於我
法中復欲何求？』</td>
    <td id='p13'>13. “I asked Sunakṣatra, ‘If I can teach you the mystery of your father, and one can use the Dharma I teach to escape from suffering, then what more do you seek from my teaching?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b23'>又告善宿：『汝先於毗舍離
<!-- [16] -->跋闍土地，無數方便，稱歎如來，稱歎正法，
稱歎眾僧。譬如有人八種稱歎彼清涼池，
使人好樂：一冷，二輕，三柔，四清，五甘，六無垢，七
飲無饜，八便身。汝亦如是，於毘舍離跋闍
土，稱歎如來，稱歎正法，稱歎眾僧，使人信
樂。</td>
    <td id='p14'>14. “I also told Sunakṣatra, ‘Back at Vaiśālī in the land of the Vṛji, you praised the Tathāgata, the correct Dharma, and the Saṅgha in countless ways. It was like someone praising the eight ways that a clear pool delights people: “It’s cool, light, gentle, clear, sweet, and clean. One can drink it without tire, and it refreshes the body.’ You were likewise. At Vaiśālī in the land of the Vṛji, you praised the Tathāgata, the correct Dharma, and the Saṅgha, which made people feel confident about them.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66b29'>善宿！當知今汝退者，世間當復有言：善
宿比丘多有知識，又是世尊所親，亦是世尊
弟子，不能盡形淨修梵行，捨戒就俗處，
卑陋行。』</td>
    <td id='p15'>15. “Sunakṣatra, you should know that now you’ve reversed yourself, the world will again say, ‘The monk Sunakṣatra has many friends. He’s close to the Bhagavān, and he’s the Bhagavān’s disciple, but he can’t purely practice the religious life his entire life. He’ll abandon the precepts and return to the lay life and inferior practices.’”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c3'>梵志！當知我時備語，不順我教，捨
戒就俗。</td>
    <td id='p16'>16. “Wanderer, you should know that after I had this talk with him, he didn’t follow my instruction. He abandoned the precepts and returned to the lay life.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c4'></td>
    <td id='p17'>17. Sunakṣatra and the Nirgrantha Disciple Kalāra</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c4'>「梵志！一時，我在獼猴池側法講堂
上，時，有尼乾子，字<!-- [17] -->伽羅樓，在彼處止，人
所宗敬，名稱遠聞，多有知識，利養備具。時，
善宿比丘著衣持鉢，入毗舍離城乞食，漸
漸轉到尼乾子所。</td>
    <td id='p18'>18. “Wanderer, I was once at the Dharma meeting hall that’s beside Monkey Lake. There was a Nirgrantha disciple named Kalāra who had stopped there.<sup id="ref4"><a href="#n4">4</a></sup> He was respected by people, and his fame was far-reaching. He had many friends who provided him with offerings. At the time, the monk Sunakṣatra had put on his robe and took his bowl into Vaiśālī to solicit alms. He made his way on the alms round until he met this Nirgrantha disciple.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c8'>爾時，善宿以深遠義問
尼乾子，彼不能答，便生瞋恚。善宿自念：『我
觸嬈此人，將無長夜有苦惱報耶？』</td>
    <td id='p19'>19. “Sunakṣatra then asked the Nirgrantha disciple about a profound subject. He couldn’t answer and became angry. Sunakṣatra thought to himself, ‘I’ve bothered this man. Won’t this lead to a result of suffering that will last a long time?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c10'>梵志！當
知時善宿比丘於乞食後，執持衣鉢，來至
我所，頭面<!-- [18] -->禮足，在一面坐，善宿爾時亦不
以此緣告我，我語之曰：『愚人！汝寧可自稱
為沙門釋子耶？』</td>
    <td id='p20'>20. “Wanderer, you should know that when the monk Sunakṣatra was finished soliciting alms, he took his robe and bowl and returned to me. He bowed to my feet and sat to one side. Sunakṣatra then told me about what had happened. I said to him, ‘You fool! Wouldn’t you rather call yourself an ascetic Śākyan disciple?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66bc14'>善宿尋報我言：『世尊！何故
稱我為愚？不應自稱為釋子耶？』</td>
    <td>“Sunakṣatra quickly replied, ‘Why do you call me a fool, Bhagavān? Shouldn’t I call myself a Śākyan disciple?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c15'>我告之
曰：『愚人！汝曾往至尼乾子所問深遠義，彼
不能報，便生瞋恚。汝時自念：我今觸此尼
乾，將無長夜有苦惱報耶？汝有是念不？』</td>
    <td id='p21'>21. “I told him, ‘Fool, you’ve already gone and questioned that Nirgrantha disciple about a profound subject. He couldn’t answer it, so he became angry. Then you thought to yourself, ‘Now, I’ve bothered this Nirgrantha disciple. Won’t this lead to a result of suffering that will last a long time?’ You had that thought, didn’t you?”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c18'>「善
宿白佛言：『彼是羅漢，何緣乃有此嫉恚心？』</td>
    <td>“Sunakṣatra said to me, ‘He was an arhat. What reason was there for him to feel hateful?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c20'>我時答曰：『愚人！羅漢何緣有嫉恚心？非我
羅漢有嫉恚心，汝今自謂彼是羅漢，彼有七
苦行，長夜執持。何謂七？</td>
    <td id='p22'>22. “I then answered, ‘Fool, what reason is there for an arhat to feel hateful? No arhat of mine has hateful feelings. Now, you say yourself, ‘He is an arhat.’ He practices the seven kinds of asceticism, which he has upheld for a long time. What are the seven?</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c22'>一盡形壽不著衣
裳。二盡形壽不飲酒食肉，而不食飯及與
麨麵。三盡形壽不犯梵行。四盡形壽<!-- [19] -->毗
舍離有四石塔——東名憂園塔、南名象塔、西
名多子塔、北名七聚塔；盡形不離四塔，
為四苦行，而彼後當犯此七苦行已，於毗
舍離城外命終。</td>
    <td id='p23'>23. “First, one doesn’t wear clothing for their entire life. Second, one doesn’t drink alcohol, eat meat, or eat rice or wheat flour for their entire life. Third, one doesn’t violate the religious life for their entire life. Fourth [to seventh], one doesn’t go beyond the four shrines of Vaiśālī, which are the eastern stone shrine called Mourning Park, the southern stone shrine called Elephant, the western stone shrine called Many Children, and the northern stone shrine called Seven Copses.<sup id="ref5"><a href="#n5">5</a></sup> They do this their entire life. This makes four ascetic practices. He’ll violate these seven ascetic practices and die while living outside of Vaiśālī.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.66c28'>譬如野干疥癩衰病，死丘塚
間，彼尼乾子亦復如是；自為禁法，後盡犯
之。本自誓言：盡形不著衣服，後還著衣。本
自誓言：盡形壽不飲酒噉肉，不食飯及麨
麵，而後盡食。本自誓言：不犯梵行，而後亦
犯。本言：不越四塔——東憂園塔、南象塔、西多子
塔、北七聚塔；今盡遠離不復親近。彼人自違
此七誓已，出毘舍離城，塚間命終。』</td>
    <td id='p24'>24. “‘That Nirgrantha disciple will be like a jackal weakened by a skin disease when he dies on a burial mound in a cemetery. He’ll violate all his own teaching’s rules. He vowed to himself, ‘I won’t wear clothes for my entire life,’ but he’ll return to wearing clothes. He vowed to himself, ‘I won’t drink alcohol, eat meat, or eat rice or wheat flour,’ but he’ll eat all of them. He vowed to himself, ‘I won’t violate the religious life,’ but he’ll violate it. He vowed, ‘I won’t go beyond the four shrines, the eastern Mourning Park shrine, the southern Elephant shrine, the western Many Children shrine, and the northern Seven Copse shrine.’ But far from them and doesn’t stay nearby. That man will contradict his own seven vows, leave the city of Vaiśālī, and his life will end in a cemetery.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a6'>佛告善
宿曰：『愚人！汝不信我言，汝自往觀，自當知
耳。』」</td>
    <td id='p25'>25. “I told Sunakṣatra, ‘Fool, if you don’t believe what I say, go investigate it and realize it for yourself.’”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a8'>佛告梵志：「一時，比丘善宿著衣持鉢，入
城乞食，乞食已，還出城，於空塚間見尼乾
子於彼命終。見已，來至我所，頭面禮足，在
一面坐，不以此事而語我言。</td>
    <td id='p26'>26. The Buddha told the wanderer, “One day, the monk Sunakṣatra put on his robe and took his bowl into the city to solicit alms. After soliciting alms, he left the city as he returned. He saw the Nirgrantha disciple’s life had ended in an empty cemetery. Seeing that, he came to me, bowed his head at my feet, and sat to one side, but that wasn’t what he spoke to me about.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a11'>梵志！當知
我爾時語善宿曰：『云何，善宿！我先所記尼
乾子如我語不？』</td>
    <td id='p27'>27. “Wanderer, you should know, I said to Sunakṣatra, ‘How is it, Sunakṣatra? Did my prediction about that Nirgrantha disciple turn out to be true?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a13'>對曰：『如是，如世尊言。』</td>
    <td>“He replied, ‘Yes, it was as the Bhagavān said.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a13'>梵志！
當知我與善宿現神通證，而彼言：『世尊不
為我現。』</td>
    <td id='p28'>28. “Wanderer, you should know, I had demonstrated evidence of my miraculous abilities to Sunakṣatra, yet he said, ‘The Bhagavān didn’t demonstrate them for me.’</td>
  </tr>
<tr>
  <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a15'></td>
  <td class='subheading'>Sunakṣatra and the Nirgrantha Disciple Khoradattika</td>
</tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.67a15'>「又一時，我在冥寧國白土之邑，時
有尼乾子，名<!-- [1] -->究羅帝，在白土住，人所宗
敬，名稱遠聞，多得利養。時，我著衣持鉢，入
城乞食，時善宿比丘隨我後行，見究羅帝
尼乾子在糞<!-- [2] -->堆上伏舐糠糟。</td>
    <td id='p29'>29. “Another time, I was at a town of the White Land in Maineya. A Nirgrantha disciple named Khoradattika was living there at the time. He was respected by people, his fame was far-reaching, and he received many offerings. I put on my robe and took my bowl into town to solicit alms, and the monk Sunakṣatra followed me. We saw the Nirgrantha disciple Khoradattika on a mound of refuse licking up coarse food.</td>

    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69b26'>我語彼言：『云何汝等實言，戱笑懈怠是
眾生始耶？』</td>
    <td id='p85'>85. “I say to them, ‘Do you really say that frivolity and laziness are the source of sentient beings?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69b27'>彼不能報，逆問我言：『瞿曇！此事
云何？』</td>
    <td>“They aren’t able to answer, so they ask me a counter question, ‘Gautama, how is it, then?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69b28'>時我報言：『或有光音眾生喜戱笑懈
怠，身壞命終，來生此間，漸漸長大，剃除鬚
髮，服三法衣，出家修道，<!-- [15] -->便入心定三昧，
以三昧力識本所生，便作是言：彼餘眾生
不喜戱笑，常在彼處，永住不變；由我等數
喜戱笑，致此無常，為變易法。』</td>
    <td id='p86'>86. “I reply to them, ‘Sometimes, the sentient beings of the Ābhāsvara Heaven are frivolous and lazy. When their bodies break up and their lives end, they’re reborn here. They slowly grow up, cut off their hair and beard, put on the three Dharma robes, and leave home for the path. They enter a samādhi of mind through which they recall their past births, and then they make the claim, “Those other sentient beings didn’t delight in frivolity, so they always reside in that abode, abiding forever and unchanging. We’ve come to this impermanence and are subject to change because we often delighted in frivolity.”’”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c4'>如是，梵志！彼
沙門、婆羅門以是緣故，言戱笑是眾生始，
如是佛盡知之，過是亦知，知而不著，<!-- [16] -->已
不著苦、集、滅、味、過、出要，如實知之，<!-- [＊] -->已平等
觀無餘解脫，名曰如來。」</td>
    <td id='p87'>87. “Thus, wanderer, as a result of these events, those ascetics and priests say that frivolity is the source of sentient beings. The Buddha thus entirely knows this, and he also knows about what’s beyond it. He knows it but isn’t attached to it. He truly knows suffering and its formation, cessation, enjoyment, danger, and escape. He’s called ‘Tathāgata’ because he was liberated without remainder by his equal observation.”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c8'>佛告梵志：「或有沙
門、婆羅門言：『失意是眾生始。』</td>
    <td id='p88'>88. The Buddha told the wanderer, “Some ascetics and priests say, ‘Losing mindfulness is the source of sentient beings.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c9'>我語彼言：『汝
等實言：失意是眾生始耶？』</td>
    <td id='p89'>89. “I say to them, ‘Do you really say, ‘Losing mindfulness is the source of sentient beings?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c10'>彼不知報，還問
我言：『瞿曇！此事云何？』</td>
    <td>“They aren’t able to answer, so they ask me a counter question, ‘Gautama, how is it, then?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c11'>我語彼言：『或有眾生
展轉相看已，便失意，由是命終，來生此間，漸
漸長大，剃除鬚髮，服三法衣，出家修道，便
入心定三昧，以三昧力識本所生，便作是
言：如彼眾生以不展轉相看，不失意故，常
住不變；我等於彼數數相看已，便失意，致此
無常，為變易法。』</td>
    <td id='p90'>90. “I say to them, ‘Some sentient beings lose their mindfulness after watching each other, one after the other. As a result, they’re born here when their lives end. They slowly grow up, cut off their hair and beards, put on the three Dharma robes, and leave home to cultivate the path. They enter a samādhi of mind through which they became aware of their past births, and then they say, “Those sentient beings that don’t lose their mindfulness while watching each other, one after the other, always abide unchanging. We frequently watched each other and lost our mindfulness after doing so. As a result, we’ve come to this impermanence and are subject to change.”’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c17'>如是，梵志！彼沙門、婆羅門
以是緣故，言失意是眾生始，如此唯佛知
之，過是亦知，知已不著苦、集、滅、味、過、出要，
如實知之，<!-- [17] -->知已平等觀無餘解脫，故名如
來。」</td>
    <td id='p91'>91. “Thus, wanderer, as a result of these events, those ascetics and priests say that losing mindfulness is the source of sentient beings. Only a Buddha thus knows it, and he knows what’s beyond it. He knows it, but he isn’t attached to it. He truly knows suffering and its formation, cessation, enjoyment, danger, and escape. He’s called ‘Tathāgata’ because he was liberated without remainder by his equal observation.”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c21'>佛告梵志：「或有沙門、婆羅門言：『我無因
而出。』</td>
    <td id='p92'>92. The Buddha told the wanderer, “Some ascetics and priests say, ‘I arose without cause.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c22'>我語彼言：『汝等實言：本無因出耶？』</td>
    <td id='p93'>93. “I say to them, ‘Do you really say, “I arose without cause”?’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c22'>彼
不能報，逆來問我，</td>
    <td>“They aren’t able to answer, so they ask me a counter question[, ‘Gautama, how is it, then?’]</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c23'>我時報曰：『或有眾生無
想無知，若彼眾生起想，則便命終來生此間，
漸漸長大，剃除鬚髮，服三法衣，出家修道，
便入心定三昧，以三昧力識本所生，<!-- [18] -->便
作是<!-- [19] -->言：我本無有，今忽然有；此世間本無，
今有，此實餘虛。』</td>
    <td id='p94'>94. “Then I reply, ‘Some sentient beings lack conception or perception. If those sentient beings give rise to concepts, then they’re reborn here when their lives end. They slowly grow up, cut off their hair and beards, put on the three Dharma robes, and leave home to cultivate the path. They enter a samādhi of mind through which they became aware of their past births, and then they say, “I didn’t exist in the past, but now I suddenly exist. This world didn’t exist in the past, but now it exists. This is true; the rest is false.”’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.69c28'>如是，梵志！沙門、婆羅門以
此緣故，言無因出，唯佛知之，過是亦知，知
已不著苦、集、滅、味、過、出要，如實知之，已平
等觀無餘解脫，故名如來。」</td>
    <td id='p95'>95. “Thus, wanderer, as a result of these events, ascetics and priests say that they arose without cause. Only the Buddha knows it, and he knows what’s beyond it. He knows it, but he isn’t attached to it. He truly knows suffering and its formation, cessation, enjoyment, danger, and escape. He’s called ‘Tathāgata’ because he was liberated without remainder by his equal observation.”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a2'>佛告梵志：「我所
說如是，或有沙門、婆羅門於屏處誹謗我
言：『沙門瞿曇自稱弟子入淨解脫，成就淨
行，彼知清淨，不遍知淨。』</td>
    <td id='p96'>96. The Buddha told the wanderer, “I’ve explained such things, but some ascetics and priests slander me in private. They say, ‘The ascetic Gautama himself claims, “My disciples enter pure liberation and accomplish pure practice. They know purity, but they don’t completely know purity.”’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a5'>然我不作是說：
『我弟子入淨解脫，成就淨行，彼知清淨，不
遍知淨。』</td>
    <td id='p97'>97. “But I don’t make the claim, ‘My disciples enter pure liberation and accomplish pure practice. They know purity, but they don’t completely know purity.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a7'>梵志！我自言：『我弟子入淨解脫，成
就淨行，彼知清淨，一切遍淨。』」</td>
    <td id='p98'>98. “Wanderer, I myself say, ‘My disciples enter pure liberation and accomplish the pure practice. They know purity, being entirely and completely pure.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a8'>是時，梵志白
佛言：「彼不得善利，毀謗沙門瞿曇言：『沙門
自言：「我弟子入淨解脫，成就淨行，彼知清
淨，不遍知淨。」』</td>
    <td id='p99'>99. The wanderer then said to the Buddha, “They can’t obtain good benefits, so they slander the ascetic Gautama by saying, ‘The ascetic himself says, ‘My disciples enter pure liberation and accomplish pure practice. They know purity, but they don’t completely know purity.’</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a11'>然世尊不作是語，世尊自言：
『我弟子入淨解脫，成就淨行，彼知清淨，一
切遍淨。』」</td>
    <td id='p100'>100. “But the Bhagavān doesn’t say that. The Bhagavān himself says, ‘My disciples enter pure liberation and accomplish the pure practice. They know purity, being entirely and completely pure.’”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a13'>又白佛言：「我亦當入此淨解脫，成
就淨行，一切遍知。」</td>
    <td id='p101'>101. He also said to the Buddha, “I’ll also enter this pure liberation and accomplish pure practice. I’ll entirely and completely know it!”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a14'>佛告梵志：「汝欲入者，甚
為難也。汝見異、忍異、行異，欲依餘見入淨
解脫者，難可得也；但使汝好樂佛，心不
斷絕者，則於長夜，常得安樂。」</td>
    <td id='p102'>102. The Buddha told the wanderer, “It would be very difficult for you to enter it. Your views are different, your patience is different, and your practice is different. It’s very difficult to enter pure liberation while relying on other views. If you keep delighting in the Buddha in your heart, then you’ll always be happy for a long time.”</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td class='ch' title='t1.1.70a17'>爾時，房伽婆
梵志聞佛所說，歡喜奉行。</td>
    <td id='p103'>103. When the wanderer Bhārgava heard what the Buddha taught, he rejoiced and approved.</td>
  </tr>
</table>
